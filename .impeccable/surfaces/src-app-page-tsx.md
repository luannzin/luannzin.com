# Home (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope: header (avatar, name, role), hero bio, lines (the one section that leads with art: the lines' dusk night tiles, user-asked to be more delightful than the rest, under the Art Exception), projects list. Visitor mode: Experience, read-like: the text and the work lead, the interface recedes.

Audience: engineers, founders, recruiters arriving from GitHub/X/LinkedIn. Job: learn who Luan is and open a project or profile. Proof: echo and better-intl, real links. Constraints: English and Portuguese, dark, light and dawn themes (system light or dark by default) picked in the dock, Central Icons, coss tokens.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A single quiet column where the copy and the real work carry the page, the lines first as the one burst of art; it refuses the hero-headline, CTA-button, skills-grid portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black neutral ground (coss dark tokens), three text tiers (foreground, foreground/70, muted), Inter at 16px, hairline underlines on links, 1.5px Central Icons in currentColor, 44px rounded project tiles with a faint ring. No accent color.

STORY: Visitor sees a face and a name, reads two short paragraphs about what Luan builds and where, then meets the lines (Deskline, Sightline) as night tiles that wake under the pointer, opens one, or goes on to the projects, writing, or the GitHub/X/LinkedIn inline links.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 576px column, top-left aligned, ~112px from top on desktop. Avatar 40px beside name/role, bio 32px below, the Lines heading ~80px below the bio, its two night tiles visible without scrolling on desktop.

FORM: Brief-pinned reference form (Krehel / Robinson / Kowalski / interfaces.dev text portfolio). No concept roll: precisely specified narrow request with pinned references. Seed key: none (pinned). Signature interaction: one staged entrance (header, bio, lines, projects and the rest, 100ms apart), the lines' dusk dots developing once their section is in; a line's glow blooms on hover or focus; project row hover reveals an arrow and brightens the underline.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
